WHAT DETERMINES YOUR PRICE?

Every Ceiling Has Different Requirements.

The cost of ceiling work depends on more than the size of the room. We look at the existing surface, the work required, and the finish you're looking for before determining the scope of the project.

01
PROJECT SCALE

Ceiling Size

The overall area of the ceiling affects the amount of preparation, removal, finishing, and materials required.

02
EXISTING SURFACE

Existing Texture

Different textures can require different removal or matching techniques and levels of preparation.

03
SURFACE CONDITION

Surface Condition

Cracks, uneven areas, previous repairs, or other surface issues may require additional refinement before finishing.

04
PROJECT PREPARATION

Preparation Required

Protecting the room and properly preparing the surrounding space are important parts of creating a clean working environment.

05
FINAL APPEARANCE

Desired Finish

The final appearance you're looking for can influence the level of surface refinement and finishing work required.
