PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CeilingCraft collects, uses, protects, and handles information when you visit our website or contact us about our ceiling services.

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

01

Information We Collect

When you contact CeilingCraft, request an estimate, schedule a visit, or submit information through our website, we may collect information that you choose to provide.

This may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, property type, approximate ceiling size, requested service, preferred visit details, and information about your project.

02

Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when communicating with CeilingCraft or submitting an inquiry through our website.

You may provide information when requesting a free estimate, asking about our services, booking a visit, sending a message, or otherwise communicating with us about your ceiling project.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to understand your project, respond to your questions, provide estimates, coordinate visits, and communicate with you regarding requested services.

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service requests.
  • Prepare or discuss project estimates.
  • Schedule and coordinate visits.
  • Communicate with you about your requested services.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
04

Communication With You

If you provide your contact information, CeilingCraft may use it to respond to your inquiry or communicate with you regarding a requested service or project.

Communications may include responses to questions, estimate-related discussions, appointment details, project coordination, and other information directly related to your request.

05

Website Usage Information

Like many websites, our website may collect certain technical information automatically when you visit.

This information may include browser type, device information, general website activity, pages visited, and other technical information used to operate and improve the website.

06

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember certain preferences, and understand how visitors interact with the website.

The use of cookies may depend on the services, analytics tools, or other technologies implemented on the website.

07

Third-Party Services

CeilingCraft may use third-party tools or services to support website functionality, communication, analytics, scheduling, hosting, maps, forms, or other business operations.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. CeilingCraft does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

08

Protection of Your Information

CeilingCraft takes reasonable steps to protect the information provided through our website and business communications.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Sharing of Information

CeilingCraft does not intend to sell your personal information as part of our normal business operations.

Information may be shared with service providers, contractors, technology providers, or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to your request, provide requested services, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

10

Retention of Information

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage projects, maintain business records, provide requested services, resolve disputes, or meet applicable obligations.

The length of time information is retained may vary depending on the nature and purpose of the information.

11

Your Choices

You may choose what information you provide when contacting CeilingCraft. However, certain information may be necessary for us to respond to an inquiry, prepare an estimate, or arrange a requested visit.

If you have questions about information you have provided to us, you can contact CeilingCraft using the contact information available on our website.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

CeilingCraft may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, business practices, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

13

Contact CeilingCraft

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, the information you have provided, or how your information is handled, please contact CeilingCraft.
